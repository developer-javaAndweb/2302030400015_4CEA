Meditation

Meditation is a practice that involves focusing your mind to achieve a calm and clear state. It can help you to reduce stress, improve your concentration, and enhance your overall well being. some sessions including morning meditation, chakra meditation etc

Mindfulness

Mindfulness is the basic human ability to be fully present, aware of where we are and what we’re doing, and not overly reactive or overwhelmed by what’s going on around us. meditation session including guided imagery , body scan meditation , mindful eating etc.
